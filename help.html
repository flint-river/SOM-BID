1. What is the Pre-Bid?

Each year, everyone picks a line. A line is your work schedule for the whole year.

People pick in seniority order. The person with the most seniority picks first. Then the next person picks, and so on.

There are two parts:

  • The Pre-Bid (this website). This is where you really make your pick. You'll get a text when it's your turn. There's no rush. Take your time and get it right.
  • The official bid date. On that day, the picks from the Pre-Bid are confirmed.

This website lets you look at every line anytime, even before it's your turn. You can see which days you work, which days you're off, and which holidays you get off.

Good to know: You can't break anything by clicking around. Looking at lines does not pick a line. Your pick goes in when you text it back on your turn.

2. The 5-minute version

  1. Open the bid page in Google Chrome.
  2. Click 💝 Build My Life. Add the dates you need off. Click Find My Perfect Lines.
  3. Click a line's Desc box (like SOM) to see its full year calendar.
  4. Click the ★ star on lines you like. They go in your ⭐ Favorites.
  5. Pick a few choices, from your favorite down. People ahead of you may take a line you like, so have backups!
  6. Wait for your text. Texts go out in seniority order, so watch the page to see who's picked.
  7. When you get your text, take your time. Then send back your pick. Your name will show up next to that line.

3. Reading the screen

The top

The top bar counts lines for SSOM and SOM:

  • Taken = lines someone already picked.
  • Avail = lines still open.

On the right you'll see a small dot and a word like Loaded. That means the page is up to date. The page updates by itself every 5 minutes. Click ⟳ Refresh to update it right now.

This shows everyone in pick order, with the line each person picked. Find your name to see your place in line.

5. The small work-day numbers

Under each line number is a tiny number. It's how many days that line works in the whole year. Our contract says 152 days.

ColorMeans
151 YellowLess than 152 days
152 GreenExactly 152 days
153 BlueMore than 152 days

Point your mouse at the small number to see a bigger box with the details, like "153 work days, Contract: 152 days, Difference: +1 day".

Some years have an extra day (a leap year), so some lines come out at 153. The company may balance this later. If that happens, these numbers update by themselves.

6. See a line's whole year

  1. Find a line you like.
  2. Click its Desc box (the colored box that says SOM or SSOM).
  3. A calendar opens with every day of the bid year. The top also shows the line's work days.

Buttons on the calendar

ButtonWhat it does
📅 Toggle ViewSwitches between two calendar styles. Pick the one that's easier for you.
🔗 Copy LinkCopies a link to this calendar so you can send it to someone, like your family.
📥 Export ICSDownloads the line so you can add it to Outlook or your phone's calendar.
✏️ YOUR EDITOpens the line in an editor where you can plan swaps, PTO, and more. See More helpful tools.
🖨️ PrintPrints the calendar.

Colors on the calendar

  • Red outline = a holiday.
  • Solid red = a balance change. That day was changed from the normal pattern.

7. Build My Life (find your best lines)

This is the best tool for finding a line that fits your life. You tell it the days you need off. It tells you which lines give you those days off.

  1. Click 💝 Build My Life at the top.
  2. One day: Pick the date. Type what it is (like "Son's graduation"). Choose Must Have Off or Prefer Off. Click + Add.
  3. Many days in a row (like a vacation): Use Add Date Range. Pick a start and end date, type what it is, then click + Add Range.
  4. Add all your important dates.
  5. Click 🔍 Find My Perfect Lines.
  6. You'll see a list of lines with a score. Lines at the top fit your dates best.

Two checkboxes help you sort the results:

  • Show only perfect matches = only lines that give you every date off.
  • Favorites first = puts your starred lines at the top.
Important: your dates are private and stay on this computer. No one else can see them, not even the bid admin. But if you switch computers or clear your browser, they're gone. Click 📥 Save to download your dates as a file. On another computer, click 📤 Load to bring them back.
Tip: Use Must Have Off only for days you truly can't work. Use Prefer Off for "nice to have" days. This gives you better results.

8. Favorites

  1. Click the ★ star next to any line you like. It turns gold.
  2. The ⭐ Favorites (3) button counts how many you've starred.
  3. Click the Favorites button to show only your starred lines. Click it again to see all lines.
Tip: Star more lines than you need. If someone ahead of you takes your top pick, you'll have backups ready.
Like Build My Life, your favorites are saved only on this computer.

9. Holidays

Click 🎄 Holidays to see a chart of which lines get each holiday off.

This is great if a certain holiday matters a lot to you, like Thanksgiving or Christmas.

Holidays move to a different pattern day each year, so check every year. For example, some years have no Easter in the bid year at all.

10. More helpful tools

ButtonWhat it does
📊 Line SummaryThe bar just above the lines. Click it to see a quick count of the lines, like how many SSOM and SOM lines there are and how many have relief days.
📅 Yearly TimelinesShows all lines for the whole year on one big chart. Good for comparing lines side by side. Use the Show menu and pick SSOM Lines Not Bid Yet or SOM Lines Not Bid Yet to see only the lines that are still open.
🔄 CompareFinds lines that are good swap partners with each other, such as "5 On / 7 Off" and "2-Day Pickup" swaps. It follows the 8-hour rest rule. Relief days (XX) count as 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and an X day can always swap straight with another X day. Click any ✓ to open that pair in Your Edit with ⚡ Auto-Swap ready.
✏️ Your EditOpens a line in an editor where you can plan your year. Add PTO, plan swaps, and download your plan. Changes here are only for you. They do not change your real bid.
Add time off: drag a box from the Time Off row (VAC, HOL, PPT, PTD = Paid Trade Day, OFF, SWAP OFF, COMP OFF) onto a day. Each one has its own color, and it counts as a day off.
Add a shift: drag a shift onto a day. A box asks what kind it is. Type the number: 1 = Regular, 2 = SWAP with a coworker (shows SW-), 3 = OT, Overtime (OT-), 4 = DT, Double Time (DT-), 5 = PTD, Paid Trade Day (PTD-). These count as work days.
Fill a lot of days fast: click any box (a shift or time off) once, then click the days you want. Hold Shift and click to fill a whole stretch. Click the box again to stop.
Plan your partner's line too: once you add a swap partner, all of these work on their row as well (time off, shifts with the 1–5 choice, specials, COMP WK, and click-to-fill). Their changes show in bold. They are saved with your plan, go into the Partner's copy you send them, and UNDO takes them back. A day that's part of a swap can't also be changed. Undo the swap first.
Click ♥ Add My Life Dates to mark your Build My Life days with a ♥. It tells you which ones are already off and which land on work days. You can fill those work days with VAC, PPT, PTD (Paid Trade Day), or another code, or just leave the ♥ marks.
⚡ Auto-Swap: add a swap partner, then click ⚡ Auto-Swap. If your lines match for a 5 On / 7 Off or 2-Day Pickup swap, it sets up every swap for the whole year for you. You pick the swap: 5 On / 7 Off can trade your 2-day blocks or your 3-day blocks, so you choose which 5 days you work. For 2-Day Pickup, your 2-day week and your partner's 2-day week sit side by side. You take turns: one cycle you work all 4 days and they are off, the next cycle they work all 4 and you are off. You pick who goes first. It shows a preview before anything changes. Important: do this before you bid vacation or make any other swaps, because both schedules must start clean. One UNDO takes it all back. Then click 📤 Partner's copy and send that file to your partner, so they can OPEN the same swaps in their Your Edit.
Save your work: click 💾 SAVE (or Ctrl+S). It downloads a small plan file. Next time, click 📂 OPEN in Your Edit, or Open Saved Plan in the Your Edit line picker, and pick that file. Everything comes back, including your swaps. Keep the file somewhere safe, because the Downloads folder may be cleared when you sign out.
Want it in Excel? Click 📊 EXCEL. It downloads an Excel file with three tabs: Year View (colored just like the page), Schedule (one row per day, showing what you changed), and Summary (work days, holidays off, and your swap balance). It's good for printing or sharing. To keep working on your plan in Your Edit later, use SAVE and OPEN, not the Excel file.
Changed your mind on a swap? Drag the shift back to where it came from, or click UNDO (or press Ctrl+Z). Both rows and the swap count go back.

11. When it's your turn

The Pre-Bid (where you really pick)

  1. Texts go out one person at a time, in seniority order. Watch the page. When the person just above you on the Seniority List has a line, you're next.
  2. You'll get a text that says it's your turn for the Pre-Bid.
  3. There's no rush. Look at the page again, because some of your favorites may be taken now. Taken lines show a name instead of Available.
  4. Pick your line from what's still open.
  5. Text back your pick.
  6. Check the page. Your name shows up next to your line. Then the next person gets their text.

The official bid date

  1. Click ⏰ View Official Bid Times to see your official date and time.
  2. On that day, the line you picked in the Pre-Bid is confirmed.
  3. Click 🔒 Bid Confirmations to see which picks are confirmed. On the main page, a confirmed pick shows a 🔒 lock.
Tip: Do your homework before your text comes. Use Build My Life and Favorites now, so you're ready when it's your turn.
If you see an orange "ESCALATION IN PROGRESS" bar at the top, some lines are being handled in a special way. Read the message in the bar, and ask the bid admin if you have questions.
